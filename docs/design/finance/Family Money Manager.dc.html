HI-FI · v1 Family Money Manager — Android, 11 screens
Family model

One shared household budget. Every member sees shared accounts, budgets and the joint feed. Each member also keeps private accounts — marked with an eye-slash, excluded from family totals, visible only to their owner. The Total switcher at the top of Home swaps between Family, a single member, or one account.

Groups → categories

Two levels. The original app's 40-icon flat grid is the main usability problem — 5 groups (Дім, Їжа, Транспорт, Здоров'я, Розваги) collapse it into a scannable list. Budgets can sit on a group or a category; the donut charts groups by default and drills into categories on tap.

Templates & widgets

Templates are per-member: Кава ₴50 logs on tap, long-press opens the add sheet prefilled. They surface as chips on Home, at the top of the add sheet, and as a 4-slot home-screen widget. Seven widget types in three sizes.

Colour departs from the reference on purpose: the source app assigns a random saturated hue per category, which at 40 categories reads as noise. Here the ramp carries eight tonal steps of one blurple family — the group is legible by value, and the accent stays an accent. One addition is proposed to the system: an overspend value #e5928a, generated in OKLCH at accent-400’s own lightness and chroma (L 0.734, C 0.125, hue 27) so it sits on the same perceptual step as the rest of the ramps. Nocturne carries no danger role, and a budget breach is the one thing in this app that must read at a glance.
